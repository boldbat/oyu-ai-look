# Sample wardrobe photos

The sample wardrobe images live in `public/assets/wardrobe/` as 1024px JPEGs.

- The six original garments (navy top, beige top, stone and black trousers, white and black sneakers) and the style photos in `public/assets/looks/` are the project's own assets, re-encoded from the PNGs in `public/assets/`.
- The elegant, old-money, evening, womenswear and menswear pieces (32 files, plus the black and floral dresses) were generated with FLUX.1 [dev] on Runpod Public Endpoints as top-down flat lays or studio product shots. Prompts asked for no people, logos or text.
- The remaining pieces are free-license stock photos, credited below.
- Every garment was cut out with macOS Vision subject lifting and placed on the app's off-white product backdrop (#fefdfb) with a soft shadow, so stock and generated photos match.
